# Pricing and market size: payroll compliance checks (בדיקת שכר) for clients of cleaning, security and catering contractors in Israel (as of Sep 2026)

> **Method note:** The egress proxy blocked direct page fetches from every Israeli domain tried (gov.il, knesset.gov.il, mevaker.gov.il, mr.gov.il, muni sites, cpa.co.il, pay-check.co.il, calcalist, etc.). All findings below come from **search-engine result snippets/summaries**, not full documents. The search tool paraphrases snippets, so treat exact figures as "reported in the snippet of source X". Before quoting any number externally, open the linked PDF and confirm it. The most valuable documents to open are the State Comptroller 2024 local-government report and the Akko 18/2025 tender.

## 1. Prices: what payroll-check firms charge

### Takeaway
No regulated or standard fee exists. Price depends on how many workers are sampled and how many violations are found. The only per-unit B2B price found in public is a vendor claim of **300 to 500 NIS to check one worker for one month**. Municipal tenders exist (e.g. Akko 18/2025), but their price figures could not be read through the snippets. Retail per-worker slip-check prices (350 NIS and up, 1,500 NIS and up per 12 slips) belong to a different, B2C market.

### Cited Findings
- A vendor page says checking one worker's pay for one month "can cost between 300 and 500 NIS". It also says the cost varies with the number of workers checked and the number of defects found — [cpa.co.il, בדיקת שכר עובדי קבלן](https://www.cpa.co.il/accounting-services/contractor-salary-audit/) (snippet only. The same snippet says periodic checks must be done by a CPA "under section 54". That conflicts with the regulations, which require a certified payroll checker (בודק שכר מוסמך), so this source's reliability is questionable.)
- "The cost of the check varies with the number of workers checked and the number of defects found in the audit." Several vendor sites repeat this — [poterit.co.il](https://poterit.co.il/periodic-inspection-by-a-payroll-inspector/); [audit-fa.co.il](https://www.audit-fa.co.il/paychecks)
- The "זכות לעובד" price list charges 270 NIS for each additional year checked, or 330 NIS for workers under extension orders or collective agreements, on top of a base price. This is a worker-side (B2C) service — [zlo.co.il מחירון](https://zlo.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%97%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%9F/)
- Another B2C price list: preliminary slip check from 350 NIS; full report (minimum 12 slips) from 1,500 NIS + VAT; or 20% of recovered wages. It appeared in snippets tied to "לשכת בודקי שכר מוסמכים" / bodeksahar.co.il — [bodeksachar.org.il](https://www.bodeksachar.org.il/); [bodeksahar.co.il](https://bodeksahar.co.il/)
- **Akko municipality, public tender 18/2025, "מתן שירותי בדיקות שכר לעובדי קבלני שירות"** (2025). The snippet shows it covers periodic payroll-check cycles for service contractors (including cleaning), handling of worker complaints, and a scoring or weighting table (40% periodic checks and complaints, 10%, 20% defect correction, 10% pension-fund audits). The price and scope were not visible in the snippet — [Akko tender 18/2025 PDF](https://www.akko.muni.il/uploads/n/1755683298.4184.pdf)
- Other 2025 municipal tender documents came up for payroll-check terms, but their content could not be verified: Eilat 2/2025 framework tender ([PDF](https://www.eilat.muni.il/uploads/n/10007.pdf)), Ra'anana 16/2025 ([PDF](https://www.raanana.muni.il/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%A8%D7%96-16-2025-%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%97-%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9-%D7%A2%D7%93%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%99-1.pdf)), Akko 14/2025 ([PDF](https://www.akko.muni.il/uploads/n/1753878349.1063.pdf))
- The Israel Institute of CPAs (לשכת רואי חשבון) sells a course called "בודק שכר – היערכות לשנת 2025". This suggests CPAs are entering the field — [icpas.org.il](https://icpas.org.il/product/%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%A7-%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8-%D7%94%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%AA-2025/)

### Inferences
- If the 300 to 500 NIS per worker-month figure applies to B2B checks, a single first ("היכרות") check can be estimated. Assumption: 10 workers sampled × 6-month reference period × 300 to 500 NIS = **18,000 to 30,000 NIS per check**. That looks high compared with the amounts recovered (see section 4). The vendor's "per worker per month" wording may really mean "per worker per check". In that case a 10-worker check costs about **3,000 to 5,000 NIS**. This needs to be checked against real tender price forms.
- Payroll checking is priced per checked worker or per check, not per client worker per month. So revenue scales with **sample size × check frequency**, not with total headcount.

### Gaps
- No actual winning bid price or maximum price from a payroll-check tender could be read. Opening the Akko 18/2025 PDF (price form, usually "נספח הצעת מחיר") is the top priority.
- No central government (מינהל הרכש) payroll-check tender was confirmed. Searches only turned up an unrelated mr.gov.il tender 8/2018 for a "בודק מוסמך" of lifting equipment. The government may handle this per ministry or through the Accountant General's (חשב כללי) instructions (הוראות תכ"ם). This is unconfirmed.
- No public price list from the large B2B checking firms (e.g. pay-check.co.il, magen-sahar.co.il) was found.

## 2. Who pays, and are there regulated caps?

### Takeaway
Under the Enforcement of Labor Laws Law (חוק להגברת האכיפה של דיני העבודה) and its periodic-check regulations, the **client (מזמין השירות) orders and pays** for the periodic check. The client gets a liability defense in return. No regulated fee or cap was found. Public bodies must use checkers. Private clients use them voluntarily, as a safe harbor from joint liability.

### Cited Findings
- The report is ordered by the service recipient (מזמין השירות), who is the party that actually receives the contractor workers' services. The payroll checker works at the client's request — [bdo.co.il guide](https://www.bdo.co.il/he-il/%D7%9B%D7%AA%D7%91%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D/%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A5-%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99/%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%94%D7%95%D7%9C-%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%95%D7%91%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%A4%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%AA/%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%9B%D7%9A-%D7%AA%D7%AA%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%95-%D7%90%D7%AA-%D7%AA%D7%A0%D7%90%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%9F-%D7%91%D7%97%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%94) (snippet)
- A client that relies in good faith on periodic checks by a certified payroll checker, and does everything it can to have a discovered violation fixed, has a defense. It may also cancel the contract over the violation. A payroll checker may not be an employee of the contractor or of the client it checks (conflict-of-interest rule) — [poterit.co.il](https://poterit.co.il/periodic-inspection-by-a-payroll-inspector/); regulation text at [Knesset regulation draft PDF](https://m.knesset.gov.il/Activity/committees/Labor/Regulation/calcala090314.pdf) and [nevo 147453](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/147453.htm)
- The law makes a client liable when a contractor in security and guarding, cleaning, catering, or other fields the minister designates breaches listed provisions (third schedule) — [kolzchut – חוק להגברת האכיפה](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%92%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%94_%D7%A9%D7%9C_%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94); [hilan.co.il chapter 6](https://www.hilan.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%96-%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2/%D7%97%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%94/%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%92%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%94-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94/%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%A7-%D7%95-%D7%AA%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A8%D7%94/)
- **Required check frequency and sample sizes (sources conflict):**
  - One snippet says the first check ("בדיקת היכרות") covers 10% of the contractor's workers or 4 workers, whichever is greater, and runs no later than 6 months after the contract starts — [bdo.co.il](https://www.bdo.co.il/) / [poterit.co.il](https://poterit.co.il/periodic-inspection-by-a-payroll-inspector/) (snippets)
  - Another snippet says the first check covers 21% or 5 workers, and a repeat check ("בדיקה חוזרת") samples 8% or 1 worker. It also says a "full repeat check of at least 21% at least once every nine months" — [Knesset regulation PDF](https://m.knesset.gov.il/Activity/committees/Labor/Regulation/calcala090314.pdf); [bazlaw summary](https://bazlaw.co.il/%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%AA%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%93-%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%A7-%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9A/)
  - A third snippet says a repeat check covers 8% of the contractor's workers since the last check — [pay-check.co.il](https://pay-check.co.il/)
  - **These sources conflict.** The Knesset PDF is a 2014 draft, and the final regulations may differ. Check the regulation text in force.
- Certified payroll checkers register with the Ministry of Labor (service "רישום כבודקי שכר מוסמכים"), and the ministry publishes a register — [gov.il service page](https://www.gov.il/he/service/registration-as-a-certified-wage-checker); [levkovich.co.il](https://levkovich.co.il/%D7%A8%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%A7%D7%99-%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%93-%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94/)

### Inferences
- In practice some public tenders may shift the cost to the contractor through contract clauses (e.g. "עלות בודק השכר תחול על הקבלן"). A targeted search found no confirmed example, so this remains unverified. The default legal model is that the client pays.
- Public bodies are a captive, mandatory market. Private clients form a voluntary safe-harbor market whose demand depends on perceived liability.

### Gaps
- No regulation sets a fee or cap. None was found, and it is likely there is none, but this is unconfirmed.
- The number of registered certified payroll checkers is not known. The register exists on gov.il but could not be counted because fetching was blocked.

## 3. Market scope: workers, contractors, clients

### Takeaway
Ministry of Labor data cited by the State Comptroller put employment at about **105,000 contractor workers in 2023**: about 54,900 in cleaning and about 50,000 in security. Estimates range from about 120,000 (Knesset research center, including home care) to 250,000 (Federation of Chambers of Commerce). No catering contractor headcount was found. There are hundreds of licensed cleaning contractors (about 839 in an older figure) and about 288 to 350 licensed security contractors (also older figures). Israel has roughly 250 local authorities.

### Cited Findings
- 2023, per Ministry of Labor data in the State Comptroller report: **50,007 contractor workers in security and guarding** and **54,882 in cleaning**. The report rounds this to about 105,000 service-contractor workers (about 55k cleaning, about 50k security) — [State Comptroller 2024, local government, "העסקת עובדים באמצעות קבלני שירות"](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2024/Shilton/2024-Shilton-204-Empoyment.pdf); [report page](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Pages/Reports/7771-6.aspx); [Calcalist coverage](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/article/sy22i5goa)
- The Knesset Research and Information Center (older, about 2015 to 2019) says Israel employs at least about 120,000 workers in cleaning, security, guarding and home care. Other documents (the Federation of Chambers of Commerce) estimate about 250,000. It also puts contractor workers in the state service at about 12,000, about 20% of directly employed state workers — [Knesset MMM doc 13582 (2019)](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/globaldocs/MMM/c3b0724d-bd1f-e911-80e1-00155d0a98a9/2_c3b0724d-bd1f-e911-80e1-00155d0a98a9_11_13582.pdf); [Knesset MMM doc 10064](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/globaldocs/MMM/eabc8d55-f7f7-e411-80c8-00155d010977/2_eabc8d55-f7f7-e411-80c8-00155d010977_11_10064.pdf) (older, pre-2020)
- Licensed contractors: snippets cite "839 cleaning companies licensed by the Ministry of Industry, Trade and Labor (משרד התמ"ת)", "288 security companies with a valid license" and "about 350 security companies". The ministry name משרד התמ"ת implies pre-2013 data, so treat these as **old**. The live lists are the Ministry of Labor's "רשימת קבלני שירות – שמירה, אבטחה וניקיון" and "קבלני כח אדם מורשים" — [gov.il service-contractors list](https://www.gov.il/he/Departments/DynamicCollectors/service-contractors-list); [manpower contractors list](https://www.gov.il/he/Departments/DynamicCollectors/manpower-contractors-list)
- The State Comptroller audited 5 local authorities (Bnei Brak, Netanya, Afula, Kiryat Yam, and Misgav regional council) from May to November 2023. **Afula and Kiryat Yam had not appointed a payroll checker, so no checks were done** — [State Comptroller 2024](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2024/Shilton/2024-Shilton-204-Empoyment.pdf); [ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/h1ksjfbuc); [mevaker press release 16.7.2024](https://www.mevaker.gov.il/newsroom/2024-07-16-local-report)
- The Comptroller's scope covered about 250 local authorities (snippet) — [davar1](https://www.davar1.co.il/525305/)
- Minimum wage for cleaning and maintenance company workers from 1.1.2026 to 31.3.2026 (extension order): 6,922.67 NIS a month for full-time, 34.32 NIS an hour — [kolzchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8_%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%9D_%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99_%D7%97%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%97%D7%96%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%94)

### Inferences
- The 105k figure (2023) most likely counts workers of licensed service contractors in cleaning and security only. With catering added (no figure found) and in-house or unlicensed arrangements, the addressable pool is probably **about 110k to 150k workers**. That is an estimate.
- Wage per worker is about 7k NIS a month, so the underlying wage bill is about 105k × about 7k × 12 ≈ **8.8 billion NIS a year** for cleaning and security alone. That is an estimate, and it is the "money under audit".

### Gaps
- No catering (הסעדה) contractor headcount was found.
- No current (2025/26) count of licensed contractors was found. The gov.il lists could not be fetched.
- No count of public bodies or large private clients using contractors was found. Only the approximate number of local authorities (~250) is known.
- No CBS (הלמ"ס) series was located in snippets.

## 4. Volume of checks and money flows (actual data points)

### Takeaway
The only hard volume figures come from the local-government sector. **Mashkal** (משכ"ל, the Company for Economy and Finance in Local Government, which runs joint tenders for local authorities) ran **1,173 payroll checks from January 2022 to mid-2023**, about 780 a year. They found **30,700 violations** and required contractors to repay **about 1.4M NIS**. That is roughly 1,200 NIS recovered per check.

### Cited Findings
- Mashkal (joint tenders for local authorities) ran 1,173 checks from the start of 2022 to mid-2023 and found 30,700 violations of contractor-worker rights in security and cleaning. Contractors were required to return 1.407M NIS to workers — [davar1](https://www.davar1.co.il/525305/); [State Comptroller 2024](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2024/Shilton/2024-Shilton-204-Empoyment.pdf)
- In 2022 there were about 19,000 violations worth about 886,000 NIS. By end of June 2023 there were another 11,700 violations worth about 521,000 NIS — [State Comptroller 2024 / snippet](https://www.mevaker.gov.il/newsroom/2024-07-16-local-report)
- Bnei Brak: 22 payroll checks from January 2020 to August 2022 found 715 violations — [State Comptroller 2024](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2024/Shilton/2024-Shilton-204-Empoyment.pdf) (snippet; one garbled snippet also mentions "819 checks in 2023", which could not be verified)
- Mashkal runs sample-based periodic payroll checks as part of its service package and operates a payroll phone hotline for workers. Its checks cover only contractors hired through Mashkal tenders. The Comptroller criticized that Mashkal's database was not used to steer checkers toward frequent violations — [mevaker / snippets](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2024/Shilton/2024-Shilton-204-Empoyment.pdf)

### Inferences
- About 780 checks a year through Mashkal alone gives a floor for the local-government segment. Authorities with their own checkers (e.g. Akko, Bnei Brak) add more on top.
- Recovery of about 1,200 NIS per check is probably below the cost of a check. The market's value proposition is therefore liability protection for the client, not wage recovery.

### Gaps
- No national figure for checks per year, and no stated total market value, was found in any source.
- Mashkal's payroll-check contract value and price per check were not found.

## 5. Demand trend: growing or shrinking?

### Takeaway
**Regulatory demand grew in 2024 to 2026.** From 1.1.2024 (new contracts) and 1.1.2025 (all contracts), regulations require every cleaning, security and catering service contract to set out the "ערך שעת עבודה" (hourly work value) and its wage components. A catering extension order came in 2025, and 2023 public-sector wage increases were extended to contractor workers. All of this raises complexity and the need for checks. No data was found showing large-scale insourcing that is shrinking the pool.

### Cited Findings
- New or renewed cleaning, catering and security contracts from 1.1.2024, and all older contracts from 1.1.2025, must comply with the "ערך שעת עבודה" regulations. These set the minimum hourly value and its components (base wage by seniority, event-dependent components, overtime and rest-day work) — [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.co.il/insights1/BRS/Enforcement_Enhancement_Regulations/); [Goldfarb](https://www.goldfarb.com/he/%D7%AA%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%92%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%94-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%91/); [wikisource regulation text](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%92%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%94_%D7%A9%D7%9C_%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94_(%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%91%D7%99_%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8_%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%91%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%90%D7%AA_%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9A_%D7%A9%D7%A2%D7%AA_%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94_%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9A_%D7%A9%D7%A2%D7%AA_%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94_%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93_%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%9F)); [ebnlaw](https://www.ebnlaw.co.il/he/q2laborlaw/)
- A first extension order for the catering sector was published on 19.3.2025 (snippet) — [ebnlaw Q2 labor law](https://www.ebnlaw.co.il/he/media-center/q2_laborlaw/)
- The Knesset Labor and Welfare Committee approved extending the wage increases and grants in the 2023 government–Histadrut framework agreement to contractor workers in security and cleaning in public bodies — snippet via [ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/byhv00vbza) / [Histadrut](https://www.histadrut.org.il/news/%D7%91%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%94-%D7%95%D7%94%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A0%D7%A7-%D7%97%D7%93-%D7%A4/)
- The public-bodies law for security and cleaning contractor workers was passed on 31.7.2013 — [kolzchut זכותון עובדי קבלן בגופים ציבוריים](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%96%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99_%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%9F_%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%99_%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%94_%D7%95%D7%94%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%91%D7%92%D7%95%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D)
- The State Comptroller (July 2024) criticized weak oversight, including authorities without payroll checkers and poor enforcement of fixes. That puts pressure toward more and better checking — [mevaker press release](https://www.mevaker.gov.il/newsroom/2024-07-16-local-report)

### Inferences
- There are tailwinds from compliance complexity (hourly-value appendix, seniority, the catering order, public-sector wage increases) and from Comptroller pressure. No evidence was found of shrinkage from insourcing in 2024 to 2026, but it was not searched in depth.
- The law lets the minister add more fields, but no new field was confirmed as added.

### Gaps
- No data was found on public-sector insourcing trends after 2020.
- No time series of checks or checker registrations was found.

## 6. Estimate of total annual spend on payroll checks (ESTIMATE, not sourced)

### Takeaway
**Estimate: about 15 to 60 million NIS a year, with a central guess of about 25 to 35M NIS.** This covers periodic payroll checks for clients of cleaning, security and catering contractors in Israel. It is a rough estimate, and the width of the range reflects the missing price data.

### Cited Findings (inputs)
- About 105,000 cleaning and security contractor workers in 2023 — [State Comptroller 2024](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2024/Shilton/2024-Shilton-204-Empoyment.pdf)
- About 780 checks a year through Mashkal for local authorities — [davar1](https://www.davar1.co.il/525305/)
- Price of 300 to 500 NIS per worker-month checked (vendor claim) — [cpa.co.il](https://www.cpa.co.il/accounting-services/contractor-salary-audit/)
- Sample sizes of 8% to 21% per check, with a full check at least every 9 months (conflicting sources) — [Knesset reg. draft](https://m.knesset.gov.il/Activity/committees/Labor/Regulation/calcala090314.pdf)

### Inferences (arithmetic; all assumptions flagged)
**Method A: worker-sample based**
- Workers in scope: 105k (cleaning and security) + about 15k catering (**assumption**) ≈ 120k.
- Share of those workers whose client actually runs checks: 50% to 70% (**assumption**). Public bodies must; many private clients do not. So about 60k to 85k workers.
- Workers sampled per year: about 2 checks a year × about 10% to 15% sample ≈ 20% to 30% of covered workers, so about 12k to 25k worker-checks a year.
- Price per worker-check (whole reference period): about 1,000 to 2,500 NIS (**assumption** built from 300 to 500 NIS per worker-month over a several-month reference period, with volume discounts).
- **Result: about 12M to 60M NIS a year.**

**Method B: check-count based**
- Local government: about 780 a year via Mashkal plus authorities with their own checkers, so about 1,000 to 1,500 checks a year (**assumption**).
- Government ministries and agencies, hospitals, universities, government companies and other public bodies: about 1,000 to 2,000 checks a year (**assumption**).
- Private clients (malls, high-tech campuses, banks, industry): about 1,500 to 3,000 checks a year (**assumption**).
- Total: about 3,500 to 6,500 checks a year × about 4,000 to 8,000 NIS per check (**assumption**; about 10 workers per check at several hundred NIS each, plus reporting) gives **about 14M to 52M NIS a year**.

**Cross-check:** payroll-check spend is roughly 0.2% to 0.7% of the underlying cleaning and security wage bill (about 8.8B NIS a year, itself an estimate). That is a plausible order of magnitude for a compliance-audit service.

### Gaps
- Every price and volume parameter above except the 105k workers and the Mashkal ~780 checks a year is an assumption. The single most useful data point to close the range is **the price form of one or more 2024 to 2026 municipal payroll-check tenders** (Akko 18/2025, Eilat 2/2025, Ra'anana 16/2025), together with the award amount and the number of contractors and workers covered.
- No source states a total market value or the number of checks nationally.
