# Supply side of payroll compliance checks for service-contractor clients in Israel (practitioners, practice, tools) — as of Sept 2026

> Method note: WebFetch and curl to almost every Israeli site (pay-check.co.il, kopelreem.com, bodeksachar.org.il, tlushdin.co.il, gov.il, wikisource, israelhayom.co.il) were **blocked by the sandbox egress proxy**. All findings below come from search-engine result snippets and summaries of those pages, not from full-page reads. Treat numbers as "as shown in the search snippet" and verify before publishing. ## 1. Who performs payroll checks (firms, practitioners, their backgrounds and size)

### Takeaway
The market is fragmented. It is made up of solo "בודק/ת שכר מוסמך/ת" practitioners and small boutique offices, usually payroll accountants (חשבי שכר), economists or CPAs, plus CPA firms and some labor-law offices that add payroll checks as a service line. I found no dominant national firm and no public figures on staff or client counts, apart from one practitioner's self-reported "400+ employers". The profession is regulated: checkers are certified by the Ministry of Labor (משרד העבודה) and listed in a public register (פנקס). A professional association also exists (לשכת/עמותת בודקי שכר מוסמכים).

### Cited Findings
**Regulatory basis and role**
- A certified payroll checker is someone the Ministry of Labor authorizes to inspect companies, employers or service contractors and labor suppliers for compliance with wage and working conditions under labor law. Their tasks include reviewing work contracts, reviewing wage systems, interviewing employees, writing reports for the service purchaser (מזמין השירות), and even giving expert testimony in court — [bodeksahar.co.il / search summary](https://bodeksahar.co.il/bodek-sahar-musmach); [audit-fa.co.il](https://www.audit-fa.co.il/paychecks)
- The role comes from the Law for Increased Enforcement of Labor Laws, 2011 (חוק להגברת האכיפה של דיני העבודה, התשע"ב-2011) — [Western Galilee College course page](https://www.wgalil.ac.il/external_studies/finance_center/payroll_checker/); [Hilan knowledge center on the law](https://www.hilan.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%96-%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2/%D7%97%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%94/%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%92%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%94-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94/%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%A7-%D7%95-%D7%AA%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A8%D7%94/). Several practitioner sites call it "a new profession since 2018", which is probably when certification actually began in practice — [magen-sahar / search summary](https://magen-sahar.co.il/); [tlushdin news item](https://www.tlushdin.co.il/news/620)
- Covered sectors: security (שמירה/אבטחה), cleaning (ניקיון), catering (הסעדה), and in some descriptions safety, maintenance and manpower (כוח אדם) — [bodeksahar.co.il](https://bodeksahar.co.il/); [magen-sahar.co.il](https://magen-sahar.co.il/)
- Why clients buy checks: a service purchaser who relied in good faith on periodic checks by a certified checker gets "liability protection" under section 27 of the law. Separately, the Labor Ministry commissioner may reduce an administrative fine (עיצום כספי) for an employer who relied on periodic checks and corrected the breach — [magen-sahar / search summary](https://magen-sahar.co.il/); [poterit.co.il](https://poterit.co.il/periodic-inspection-by-a-payroll-inspector/)
- One CPA-firm page says that under a temporary provision in section 54 of the law, periodic checks were to be done by a CPA rather than a certified payroll checker. This is likely the transitional regime before certified checkers existed (older info, date unclear) — [cpa.co.il contractor salary audit](https://www.cpa.co.il/accounting-services/contractor-salary-audit/)

Phone shown: 050-4906876 — [bodeksachar.org.il](https://www.bodeksachar.org.il/); [about page](http://www.bodeksachar.org.il/about); [GuideStar](https://www.guidestar.org.il/organization/580689529/people)

### Inferences
- Background mix: training eligibility (see §4) limits entry to senior payroll accountants, CPAs and labor lawyers. The firms found are consistent with this. Most practitioner sites are run by payroll accountants or economists acting as solo or boutique checkers. CPA firms (Kushner, Lavie, cpa.co.il, BDO) and labor-law firms offer it as an add-on line. Only a handful present as dedicated payroll-check firms (Pay-Check, Poterit, Magen Sachar).
- Typical size is 1–5 people. The only client-count figure found (Chen Ben-Shabat, "400+ employers") is self-reported and includes employer advisory, not only contractor checks.
- Demand is regulation-driven (liability shield plus fine reduction) and patchily enforced (the municipalities that skipped checks). That points to a price-sensitive market in which checks are bought as a compliance certificate ("אישור בודק שכר").

### Gaps
- **Number of registered certified checkers**: the gov.il register exists but could not be fetched (egress blocked), and no search snippet gave a count. The association's membership count was also not found.
- Staff and client counts for Pay-Check, Poterit, Magen Sachar and others were not found.
- The surnames of some practitioners (e.g. "Ilya" at bodeksahar.co.il) were not captured.

## 2. How a check is done in practice (documents, sample, time, findings, report, price)

### Takeaway
Checks follow regulations under the 2011 enforcement law. The checker requests contracts or employment notices, payslips, attendance reports, pension/provident fund and study-fund deposit reports, and proof of payment for a **sample** of the contractor's workers. The checker then cross-checks those documents against worker interviews and issues a written report to the service purchaser. Calculations are mostly done in Excel or in-house tools, with some firms using proprietary software. The most common findings are overtime, Shabbat/holiday pay, vacation accrual and pension deposits.

### Cited Findings
**Documents**
- Documents the checker relies on: employment agreement / notice to employee (הודעה לעובד), payslips, attendance reports, fund reports (vacation fund, provident and study funds), deposit confirmations and proof of wage payment, pension/provident deposit confirmations, and confirmation that amounts deducted from wages were transferred — [bodeksahar.co.il / search summary](https://bodeksahar.co.il/); [pay-check.co.il "what is a checker report"](https://pay-check.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%96%D7%94-%D7%93%D7%95%D7%97-%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%A7-%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9A/)
- The employer must give the checker full access to payroll documents, employment contracts, attendance reports and related information. Refusing to cooperate may be treated as a breach and lead to sanctions — [search summary of tlushdin/poterit/shuchman pages](https://shuchman.co.il/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%A7-%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9A)
- The checker assesses results after cross-referencing the documents with information from the contractor's workers, including by **interviewing workers** (תשאול עובדים) — [bodeksahar.co.il / search summary](https://bodeksahar.co.il/)

**Check types and sample size (conflicting sources)**
- Draft regulations (Knesset Labor Committee, March 2014) set **5 check types**, including an "acquaintance check" (בדיקת היכרות) covering the period from contract signing to the start of checks — [Knesset regulations draft PDF](https://m.knesset.gov.il/Activity/committees/Labor/Regulation/calcala090314.pdf)
- Sample size: acquaintance check = **10% of the contractor's workers or 4 workers**, whichever is higher. Follow-up check = **8% or 2 workers**, whichever is higher — [search summary citing regulations / heshbonatun / poterit](https://www.heshbonatun.co.il/articleView.aspx?aid=783); **contradicted by** [bodeksahar.co.il](https://bodeksahar.co.il/), whose snippet says **21% or 5 workers** for the acquaintance check. The conflict is unresolved: the final regulation text on wikisource could not be fetched. It may be a typo, or a difference between the draft and final text.
- Checks are periodic. Service purchasers must also reconcile quarterly with contractors on wage payments (search summary) — [cpa.co.il](https://www.cpa.co.il/accounting-services/contractor-salary-audit/)

**Pricing**
- Checking one worker's pay for one month "may cost ₪300–500", and the cost depends on the number of workers sampled and defects found — [search summary of cpa.co.il / poterit / magen-sahar pages](https://www.cpa.co.il/accounting-services/contractor-salary-audit/)
- B2C comparison: Zchut La'oved (zlo.co.il) charges **₪1,490 for a check of up to 3 years, plus ₪270 per additional year** from year 4 — [zlo.co.il price list](https://zlo.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%97%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%9F/)

**Typical findings (common violations)**
- Common errors: overtime miscalculated contrary to law, wrong or partial pay for Shabbat and holiday hours, low accrual of vacation days — [audit-fa.co.il blog "common violations"](https://www.audit-fa.co.il/post/%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%A7-%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9A-%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%90%D7%99%D7%95-%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A0%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94); [kolhamas guide](https://www.kolhamas.co.il/%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%A7-%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9A-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%9D-%D7%9C%D7%91%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%95/)
- A worker-side service reports these figures from its own caseload: sick pay not paid legally in 20% of cases, pension not deposited legally in 90%, vacation not accrued legally in 90%. These are self-reported by a vendor and not a representative sample. The exact site behind the figures is unclear; it appeared with [zlo.co.il](https://zlo.co.il/)
- Israel Hayom (2026) reported error frequencies from an unnamed payroll company. Vacation-day errors (calendar vs work days) fell from 9.2% in 2025 to 5.3% in H1 2026. Overtime netted against missing hours contrary to law fell from 10.2% to 6.2%. Wrong 42-hour week distribution (hours split evenly instead of one short day) fell from 9.5% to 4.6%. Sick-day counting errors fell 47.3%. The company credits AI tools used by payroll accountants but says the error rate "is still high and does not allow abandoning human review" — [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/business/article/21137565?amp=1)
- Sector minimum wages checkers must apply in 2026: security worker ₪7,118.85/month from 1 April 2026, supervisor ₪7,126.85. Cleaning worker ₪6,922.67 from 1 January 2026, cleaning supervisor ₪6,930.67 — [Kol Zchut security](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8_%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%9D_%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99_%D7%A9%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%94_%D7%95%D7%90%D7%91%D7%98%D7%97%D7%94); [Kol Zchut cleaning](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8_%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%9D_%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99_%D7%97%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%97%D7%96%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%94)
- Cleaning-sector overtime rules (extension order): 125% for hours 9–10 and 150% for hours 11–12 — [Kol Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%92%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%9C_%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A8_%D7%A9%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99_%D7%97%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%97%D7%96%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%94)

**Report**
- The output is a written "דוח בודק שכר מוסמך" submitted to the service purchaser. Some practitioners also market an "אישור בודק שכר מוסמך" (certificate) — [pay-check.co.il](https://pay-check.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%96%D7%94-%D7%93%D7%95%D7%97-%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%A7-%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9A/); [poterit.co.il](https://poterit.co.il/certified-payroll-examiner-certification/); [asmachta.co.il](https://asmachta.co.il/certified-wage-checker/)

### Inferences
- The work is a rules engine applied to heterogeneous inputs: general law plus a sector extension order (צו הרחבה), per-worker attendance, and payslip lines. It is repeated per worker and per month across a sample. At ₪300–500 per worker-month, a 10-worker, 3-month sample implies roughly ₪9K–15K per check. This is my extrapolation, not a quoted price.
- The main data burden is collecting documents from the contractor, not the purchaser. Attendance data is the weak link because the checker must validate it through worker interviews.

### Gaps
- Time per check was not found in any source.
- Standard report templates were not found (no public sample report could be fetched).
- The exact final sample-size rule (10%/4 vs 21%/5) is unresolved.
- No survey of which tools checkers actually use (Excel vs software) was found. Only Pay-Check explicitly claims proprietary software.

## 3. Software: payroll-check / labor-law compliance tools, payroll-vendor modules, AI startups

### Takeaway
No Israeli product is purpose-built for the statutory certified-checker workflow on contractor payrolls: sampling, document intake, sector extension orders, and the regulated report. The nearest tools fall into three groups. The first is B2B payroll-control tools that check the employer's own payroll before issue: KoCheck (Kopel Reem), Hilan's payroll-control and BI, Malam's payroll-control module, and Celery. The second is AI payslip analyzers aimed at lawyers, employers and workers: Posternik, PayrollAI and Tlushdin. The third is Pay-Check's in-house calculator. The AI-native players are small, and the best-funded (Celery, $9M total) targets employer payroll control, not third-party contractor checks.

- **PayrollAI (payrollai.co.il)**: "a smart system for analyzing and controlling payslips and attendance reports using AI". - **Tlushdin (tlushdin.co.il / pro.tlushdin.co.il)**: online payslip checking with a free salary simulator and rights calculators, lawyer referral and Q&A. ### Inferences
- **White space**: existing tools serve (a) the employer checking its own payroll before issuing it (KoCheck, Hilan, Malam, Celery) or (b) the worker or lawyer reconstructing a claim (Posternik, Tlushdin, law-net, zlo). None found covers the statutory **third-party check of a contractor on behalf of the service purchaser**, which involves: document requests to the contractor, sample selection per the regulations, sector extension-order rules for cleaning and security, worker-interview capture, the regulated report format, and portfolio tracking across many contractors and sites. Posternik is closest, since it has a contractor-worker post and attendance parsing, and is the one to watch.
- Large payroll vendors (Hilan, Malam, Synerion, Michpal) sit on the contractor's own data. A module that exports a "checker package" to the purchaser's checker is plausible, but I found no evidence that one exists.
- AI fits these parts of the workflow: OCR and extraction of heterogeneous payslips and attendance exports; recomputation under the extension orders; anomaly flags (overtime netting, Shabbat premium, vacation accrual, pension deposit gaps); and report drafting.

### Gaps
- Michpal (מיכפל): no compliance-check module was found; the search did not surface it.
- Synerion: only a payroll-calculation page was found, with no compliance module.
- Pricing, customer counts and founding details for Posternik, PayrollAI and Tlushdin were not retrieved (pages blocked).
- The unnamed company behind the Israel Hayom error-rate statistics is not identified.

## 4. Training: "בודק שכר" courses (providers, cost, duration, entry requirements)

### Takeaway
Many providers run Ministry of Labor-supervised certified-checker courses: academic colleges' continuing-education units, private accounting and tax colleges, and online options. Entry is restricted to senior payroll accountants, CPAs and labor lawyers, sometimes also internal auditors and economists. Length ranges from about 40–48 hours for CPAs and lawyers to about 150 hours for payroll accountants. One price data point is about ₪3,700 plus ₪400 registration. The wide spread of providers suggests ongoing supply growth.

### Inferences
- At least 10 providers run the course, several online, and it is short and cheap for CPAs and lawyers. So supply is not the constraint. More likely constraints are demand, enforcement, and the checkers' own throughput.

- Annual number of graduates or new registrations was not found.

## 5. Adjacent: worker-facing payslip checks (B2C) and pain points practitioners mention

### Takeaway
There is an active B2C and legal-lead market for worker payslip checks: Tlushdin, Koach La'oved, Zchut La'oved, law-net's "10-minute check", BiroClick/Paywatch for teachers, and Posternik's lawyer service. Most monetize through flat fees or contingency and lawyer referrals. No union-run (Histadrut) payslip-check app was found. Practitioner pain points appear only indirectly: contractors are slow or reluctant to supply documents (hence the legal duty to cooperate), and attendance data must be verified through worker interviews.

- Document access is a recurring issue. ### Inferences
- B2C tools like Tlushdin, Posternik and PayrollAI already use the same core engine (payslip + attendance → recompute → gap report). A B2B checker tool would compete with them for the engine but not for the customer, and could license or partner.
- The customer-side pain on the service-purchaser side (municipalities, public bodies, companies) is tracking findings and remediation across contractors over time, not only the one-off calculation.
