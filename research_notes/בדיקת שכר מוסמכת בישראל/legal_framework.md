# Legal framework for payroll compliance checks (בדיקת שכר) of service contractors in Israel (as of Sept 2026)

> **Method note, important for the report writer.** Every page fetch (WebFetch) was blocked by the network egress proxy: nevo, Wikisource, gov.il, Knesset, kolzchut, hilan, law-firm sites. **All findings below come from web-search result snippets and the search engine's summaries of those pages, not from reading the primary texts directly.** Section numbers, amounts and timelines should be confirmed against the law text (nevo 117033 / Wikisource) before anyone relies on them in a legal claim. ## Q1. The statute: חוק להגברת האכיפה של דיני העבודה, התשע"ב-2011. Client liability, "reasonable measures", private vs public clients, added sectors

### Takeaway
The law makes a "מזמין שירות" (service client) of a cleaning, guarding/security or catering contractor civilly liable, and in some cases administratively and criminally liable, for the contractor's violations of listed worker rights. The payroll check is **not an absolute legal duty for a private client**. It is the main statutory **defence (safe harbour)**: the client is protected if it relied in good faith on periodic checks by a בודק שכר מוסמך and acted to get violations fixed. The checks become effectively mandatory through government, local-authority and tender rules, and through the 2023 hourly-value regulations.

- Definition: "מזמין שירות" means anyone who receives a service from a contractor in the course of his business, profession **or public activity**. The definition therefore covers both private businesses and public bodies. — [search summary of nevo law text](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/117033.htm)
- **Section 25(a): civil liability** of a client of guarding, security and cleaning contractors when the section's conditions are met. — [biber-law ruling summary](https://www.biber-law.co.il/work-laws/%D7%9E%D7%96%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9F-%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%99%D7%91-%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%96%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93-%D7%A0/). Other summaries list the covered sectors as **cleaning, catering and guarding/security**. — [cpa.co.il](https://www.cpa.co.il/accounting-services/contractor-salary-audit/), [pay-check.co.il](https://pay-check.co.il/)
- **Duty of reasonable measures and complaint channel:** the client must take reasonable measures, in the circumstances, to prevent violations of the rights of the contractor's workers employed at its site. It must set an effective way at the workplace for reporting violations and handling complaints, and must inform the contractor's workers about it. — [search summary of nevo](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/117033.htm); [kolzchut page on the complaint-channel duty](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%A2%D7%9C_%D7%9E%D7%96%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9F_%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%9C%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A2_%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%9A_%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%94_%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9B%D7%9C%D7%95_%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99_%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%9F_%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%92%D7%99%D7%A9_%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%95_%D7%AA%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%A2%D7%9C_%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%AA_%D7%96%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%94%D7%9D)
- **Section 27: defences** (the "protection" a client can prove). In a civil claim for breach of a section 25 duty, it is a good defence for the client to prove **any** of the following:
  - the violation was fully corrected; or
  - it relied in good faith on **periodic checks carried out by a בודק שכר מוסמך under Chapter ו**, and on discovering a violation did everything in its power to have the contractor fix it, and if the violation was not fixed within a reasonable time, it **cancelled the contract**.
  — [search summaries of nevo / hilan](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/117033.htm), [liatfaigel-law](https://liatfaigel-law.co.il/%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%9A-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%9F-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94/article7/)
- **Section 28: contract terms.** The client must ensure that certain conditions are written into the contract with the contractor. If they are missing, civil liability applies even if other conditions are not met. — [search summary of kolzchut/dt-law](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%94%D7%92%D7%A9%D7%AA_%D7%AA%D7%91%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%94_%D7%A0%D7%92%D7%93_%D7%9E%D7%96%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9F_%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%91%D7%A9%D7%9C_%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%96%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%A9%D7%9C_%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99_%D7%94%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%9F_%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%90%D7%A6%D7%9C%D7%95) *(low confidence on the exact section number; it comes from a search-engine summary.)*
- **Administrative route:** when a contractor is fined, the Labor Ministry supervisor may warn the client. If within 30 days the violation is not corrected, or the client does not act in good faith to terminate the contract and realize the contractor's guarantee, a monetary sanction may be imposed on the client for the same violation. — [BAZ law firm, "עיצום כספי"](https://bazlaw.co.il/monetary-fine/), [workrights.co.il](https://www.workrights.co.il/%D7%94%D7%92%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%90%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%94)

- **Additional sectors:** summaries say the administrative regime covers "security, cleaning, catering **and additional sectors as decided by the Minister**", and that the law's application could be extended in future. — [search summary of hilan/kolzchut](https://www.hilan.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%96-%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2/%D7%91%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%A1-%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2/%D7%9E%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D/%D7%94%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%92%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%94-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94/). ### Inferences
- **How mandatory is it?** For a **private** client the payroll check is legally a safe harbour, not a direct obligation. A client that skips it is not automatically in breach. It loses its best defence and is exposed to worker claims, administrative sanctions after a warning, and possibly criminal liability. In practice this makes checks close to mandatory for risk-averse clients: supermarkets, hospitals, universities, high-tech campuses, malls.
- The defence is built into primary legislation (a Knesset statute, not a directive), and the complaint-channel duty was enforced by the National Labor Court in 2023/24 (Shufersal, see Q5). That makes the regime durable. Repealing the defence would **increase** client liability, which would not reduce demand for checks.
- I found no evidence of an order adding sectors beyond cleaning, guarding/security and catering. The ministerial power appears to exist but, per current practitioner sites, is unused.

### Gaps
- I could not read the exact text of sections 24–30 (all primary-text pages were blocked). Items still unconfirmed:

  - Whether there is a separate "גוף ציבורי" provision with stricter (near no-fault) liability, or a defence condition that the contract price was not lower than the minimum wage cost. From background knowledge the law does treat public bodies more strictly, but I could not confirm it.

## Q2. Who is a "בודק שכר מוסמך" (certified payroll checker)

### Takeaway
A בודק שכר מוסמך is a person recognized by the Ministry of Labor (משרד העבודה) under Chapter ו of the law and the **תקנות להגברת האכיפה של דיני העבודה (בודק שכר מוסמך), התשע"ז-2017**. Recognition requires meeting threshold conditions (payroll professional with the ministry's senior certificate, CPA, labor lawyer, or tax advisor with experience), completing a supervised course of up to about 150 hours, and passing the ministry's exams. Recognized checkers are listed in a public registry (פנקס בודקי השכר) kept by the ministry. I found no public count of registered checkers.

- A professional body exists: "לשכת בודקי שכר מוסמכים בישראל". — [bodeksachar.org.il](https://www.bodeksachar.org.il/)
- Contradicting claim: one search summary said "per a provision in the law, periodic audits must be conducted by a CPA rather than an authorized salary auditor". This conflicts with every other source, which say checks are done by a בודק שכר מוסמך. It probably reflects older government practice, where external CPAs audited contractor payslips (see Q4). **Treat it as unreliable.** — [cpa.co.il](https://www.cpa.co.il/accounting-services/contractor-salary-audit/)

### Inferences
- The certifying authority is the Ministry of Labor (currently משרד העבודה, formerly within משרד הכלכלה). Enforcement is by מינהל הסדרה ואכיפה.
- Entry routes include חשבי שכר (payroll clerks) with the senior certificate, not only CPAs or lawyers. The supply of checkers is therefore relatively broad, which bears on price competition.

### Gaps
- **Number of registered checkers:** I found no source with the count. The registry can be queried on gov.il, but fetches were blocked. Recommend a manual count from the gov.il professionals database.
- I could not confirm registration fees, renewal requirements or continuing-education duties.

## Q3. What a periodic check must contain: frequency, sample, rights checked, report, follow-up

### Takeaway
Periodic checks are governed by the regulations on periodic checks by a certified checker. The widely cited figures below come from practitioner sites; I could not verify them against the regulation text.

### Cited Findings
- Periodic checks are made under regulations issued under the law. A search summary cites them as the 2017 regulations issued under section 40 of the law. They set the frequencies, times, methods and scope of checks, the documents the checker relies on, and a **maximum of four months** to carry out a check. — [search summary of nevo 501_652](https://nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/501_652.htm), [heshbonatun "תדירות הבדיקות"](https://www.heshbonatun.co.il/pageBox.aspx?control=modulesElearning/articleView_New&aid=783)
- **Types of check:**
  - **בדיקת היכרות** (initial familiarization check): the first full check during the contract, covering the period up to **6 months** from the start of the contract.
  - **בדיקה חוזרת** (repeat check): a full check covering the period up to **one year** after the start of the previous check.
  - **בדיקה בעקבות גילוי ליקויים** (check following discovered defects): a re-check of the deficiencies found in a prior check.
  - **Pension deposits** are checked **once a year**.
  — [search summary of nevo 147453 / Knesset regs PDF / poterit](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/147453.htm), [poterit](https://poterit.co.il/periodic-inspection-by-a-payroll-inspector/)
- **Sample:** at least **10% of the contractor's workers in the check period, or 4 workers, whichever is higher**. — [search summary of bazlaw/kolhamas/poterit](https://bazlaw.co.il/%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%AA%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%93-%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%A7-%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9A/), [kolhamas](https://www.kolhamas.co.il/%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%AA%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%93-%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%A7-%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9A/)
- **Frequency conflict:** one practitioner summary says a check is needed "at least once every **9 months**". Another says repeat checks cover up to **one year** after the previous check. — [bodeksahar/bazlaw summary](https://bodeksahar.co.il/) vs [poterit / nevo summary](https://poterit.co.il/periodic-inspection-by-a-payroll-inspector/). **Needs verification against the regulation text.**
- The Accountant General's own checks in local authorities (2020–2023) also used a sample of **at least 10% of employees**. — [State Comptroller, local government 2024 report](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2024/Shilton/2024-Shilton-204-Empoyment.pdf) (via search summary)
- **Rights checked:**
  - wage components and minimum wage;
  - overtime pay and hours;
  - vacation pay (Annual Leave Law 1951);
  - recuperation pay (הבראה);
  - holiday pay;
  - travel allowances;
  - pension deposits and severance provisions;
  - severance compensation;
  - youth employment law;
  - extension orders (pension, recuperation, travel).
  In practice these follow the Third Schedule plus sector collective agreements and extension orders (cleaning, guarding). — [search summary of poterit / nevo / Knesset PDF](https://poterit.co.il/periodic-inspection-by-a-payroll-inspector/)
- **Report:** a report by the certified checker is delivered to the client. The client's defence depends on "having periodic checks by a certified checker and **producing a checker's report** stating the contractor is not violating labor laws". — [pay-check.co.il](https://pay-check.co.il/), [magen-sahar](https://magen-sahar.co.il/%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A4%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%96%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9F-%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%AA-%D7%AA/). One vendor quotes a turnaround of up to 30 business days from order (a commercial claim). — [bodeksahar.co.il](https://bodeksahar.co.il/)
- **Findings:** the statute requires that on discovering a violation the client "do everything in its power" to have the contractor fix it, and cancel the contract if it is not fixed within a reasonable time (section 27). — [nevo summary](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/117033.htm)
- **Related 2023 regulations: "ערך שעת עבודה".** These are the "תקנות להגברת האכיפה של דיני העבודה (רכיבי השכר המרכיבים את ערך שעת עבודה וערך שעת עבודה לעובד קבלן), התשפ"ג-2023". They require:
  - a minimum hourly value in the contract with cleaning, guarding/security and catering contractors, derived from the worker's base pay, and updated when the minimum wage rises;
  - a reconciliation (התחשבנות) between client and contractor **at least once per quarter, per worker**, for pay components affected by seniority and by length of the workday.
  For contracts that already existed, they apply from **1.1.2025**, or earlier if the contract is updated before then. — [search summary of goldfarb/shibolet/hilan/haifachamber](https://www.shibolet.com/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%92%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%94-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94/), [haifachamber](https://haifachamber.org.il/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%92%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%94-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%93%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94-%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%91%D7%99/), [nevo 217846](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/217846.htm), [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.co.il/insights1/BRS/Enforcement_Enhancement_Regulations/)
- gov.il offers a "מחשבון לחישוב ערך שעת עבודה" (hourly-value calculator). — [gov.il service](https://www.gov.il/he/service/calculator-work-hour)

### Inferences
- The 2023 hourly-value regulations add a second compliance layer on top of the periodic checks: contract pricing plus quarterly reconciliation. It is plausible they raise demand for payroll or accounting services around contractor contracts, but no source says the reconciliation must be done by a certified checker.
- Using the 10%-or-4 sample, a contractor with 40 workers at a site needs at least 4 workers checked. With 200 workers, 20.

### Gaps
- I could not verify these directly against the regulation text:
  - the exact frequency (9 months vs 12 months) and timing windows;
  - any prescribed report form (טופס);
  - statutory deadlines for contractor correction;
  - whether the checker or client must report findings to the Ministry.
- The exact official names of the periodic-check regulations are uncertain (possibly "(בדיקות תקופתיות בידי בודק שכר מוסמך)", 2017, under section 40). Confirm on [nevo 147453](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/147453.htm) / [nevo 501_652](https://nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/501_652.htm).

## Q4. Government, public-body and tender requirements (תכ"ם, Accountant General, local authorities, licensing)

### Takeaway
Public clients have their own layer of mandatory controls:
- **Government ministries:** the Accountant General's תכ"ם directives and a dedicated Accountant General unit for enforcing contractor workers' rights, which runs sample payroll checks and a hotline.
- **Local authorities:** Interior Ministry circular 9/2016 requires a binding internal procedure, including payroll checks.
- **Tenders:** public tenders routinely require checks.
Separately, cleaning and guarding contractors must hold a Ministry of Labor **רישיון קבלן שירות** with a bank guarantee.

### Cited Findings
- **תכ"ם on labor cost:** directive ה.7.3.9.2.3 ("עלות שכר…") sets the minimum employer cost per work hour for government service contracts and is updated periodically. Universities and colleges publish the תכ"ם cost tables in their tenders. — [kyma.co.il](https://www.kyma.co.il/7-3-9-2-3/), [BIU tender PDF](https://tiful.biu.ac.il/sites/tiful/files/michrazim/%D7%94%D7%95%D7%93%D7%A2%D7%AA-%D7%AA%D7%9B%D7%9D-%D7%A2%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%A7-%D7%9E%D7%94%D7%93%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%94-26.pdf), [AAC tender table Nov 2019](https://www.aac.ac.il/tenders/wp-content/uploads/sites/16/2020/02/%D7%98%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%AA-%D7%A2%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%99%D7%93-%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%91%D7%A8-2019-1.pdf)
- **Accountant General enforcement unit** (the "המחלקה/היחידה לאכיפת זכויות עובדים" in אגף החשב הכללי):
  - it runs periodic sample payroll checks and a wage hotline;
  - in 2022 its sample checks found about **19,000 violations**, mostly monetary, totalling about **₪886,000**;
  - between 2020 and 2023 it carried out payroll checks for local authorities' contractors, sampling at least 10% of workers.
  — [State Comptroller 2024 local-government report](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2024/Shilton/2024-Shilton-204-Empoyment.pdf) (via search summary); [Davar: "החשב הכללי באוצר: הפרות של מיליונים"](https://www.davar1.co.il/535880/)
- The State Comptroller found **deficiencies in local authorities' supervision** of contractor employment, exposing workers to significant wage violations. Davar reported the Comptroller found "**more than 30,000 violations**". — [ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/h1ksjfbuc), [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/article/sy22i5goa), [Davar 525305](https://www.davar1.co.il/525305/)
- Earlier State Comptroller reports: "העסקת עובדים באמצעות קבלני שירות" and a Nov 2022 employment report. In government offices, controls include "ongoing review by the ministry's accountant and audit of payslips by an external CPA". — [mevaker 7771-6](https://www.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Pages/Reports/7771-6.aspx), [mevaker 2022.11 PDF](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2022/2022.11/2022.11-102-Employment.pdf), [mevaker "פעולות המדינה להגנה על זכויותיהם…"](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Pages/Reports/297-25.aspx?AspxAutoDetectCookieSupport=1)
- **Local authorities:** Interior Ministry Director-General circular **9/2016** (December 2016) requires each authority's director-general to prepare a **binding internal procedure** on employing contractor workers and protecting their rights, especially in cleaning and guarding. It includes a requirement for a certified payroll checker. — [search summary of cbalaw / Comptroller](https://www.cbalaw.co.il/3513)
- **Knesset Research Center** papers on contractor employment in the public sector. — [MMM 10064](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/globaldocs/MMM/eabc8d55-f7f7-e411-80c8-00155d010977/2_eabc8d55-f7f7-e411-80c8-00155d010977_11_10064.pdf), [MMM 7285](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/globaldocs/MMM/2b4c6b58-e9f7-e411-80c8-00155d010977/2_2b4c6b58-e9f7-e411-80c8-00155d010977_11_7285.pdf)
- **Service-contractor licence** (under חוק העסקת עובדים על ידי קבלני כוח אדם):
  - cleaning and guarding/security contractors need a Ministry of Labor **רישיון קבלן שירות**;
  - conditions include at least 1 year of business or management experience, no criminal record in the last 5 years, a personal interview, a basic labor-law exam, a suitable office, and a **bank guarantee**;
  - per a 2026 law-firm checklist, the minimum guarantee as of January 2026 is **₪47,563** even with no employees, and it scales with headcount;
  - licences are renewed via gov.il.
  — [law-sabag 2026 checklist](https://law-sabag.co.il/cleaning-contractor-license-checklist/), [melamedlaw](https://www.melamedlaw.co.il/%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%9F-%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%99-%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%A9%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%94-%D7%95%D7%90%D7%91%D7%98%D7%97/), [gov.il licence renewal](https://www.gov.il/he/service/manpower-contractor-license-renewal); Employment Order: [צו העסקת עובדים על ידי קבלני שירות](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/Law01/500_917.htm)
- kolzchut has a dedicated "זכותון עובדי קבלן בתחומי השמירה והניקיון בגופים ציבוריים" (rights guide for contractor workers in guarding and cleaning at public bodies), which indicates a distinct public-body regime. — [kolzchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%96%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99_%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%9F_%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%99_%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%94_%D7%95%D7%94%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%91%D7%92%D7%95%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D)

### Inferences
- The state itself does payroll checks for government and some local-authority contracts, through the Accountant General unit. Recurring Comptroller findings of weak supervision suggest the state's capacity is limited, so public bodies keep hiring private certified checkers.
- Public tenders requiring periodic checks make the check contractually mandatory for public clients. This is a steady source of demand, independent of the private-client safe-harbour logic.

- I could not identify the exact תכ"ם directive number that mandates certified payroll checks for government contracts. Candidates are directives within chapter 7.3 / 7.11 of the תכ"ם; the snippets only covered the labor-cost directive ה.7.3.9.2.3.
- I could not read the text of Interior Ministry circular 9/2016 (frequency, who pays for the checker). I also did not find whether a newer circular replaced it.
- I could not find the Accountant General unit's 2024–2026 statistics.

## Q5. Enforcement: sanctions on clients and contractors, statistics, court rulings

### Takeaway
Administrative sanctions come in three tiers and are multiplied per worker. Clients can be sanctioned after a 30-day warning. Courts have enforced both the substantive liability and the complaint-channel duty. The leading recent National Labor Court ruling found Shufersal breached its duties toward cleaning-contractor workers.

### Cited Findings
- **Sanction tiers:** monetary sanctions come in three levels, **₪5,500 / ₪21,990 / ₪38,480**, each **per violation times the number of workers**. — [BAZ law firm](https://bazlaw.co.il/monetary-fine/). Another summary says a client may be treated as having violated the minimum-wage obligation and face a sanction of up to **₪42,100**. — [search summary of dt-law / kolzchut](https://www.dt-law.co.il/articles.asp?ArticleID=9626). **Figures conflict, probably because of different indexation years (the base amounts are linked to the CPI). Verify current amounts.**
- Clients are sanctioned only after a warning and 30 days without correction or good-faith termination of the contract. — [BAZ](https://bazlaw.co.il/monetary-fine/)
- **Shufersal ruling, National Labor Court, ע"ע 46450-01-21, Beyene v. Shufersal Ltd.:**
  - the claim concerned cleaning workers employed through the contractor "Super Service for All Ltd.";
  - the court held Shufersal failed its duty to set an effective way for contractor workers to complain and to inform them of it;
  - it said reasonable steps include, for example, translating the complaint procedure into a language the workers understand.
  — [afiklaw case page](https://he.afiklaw.com/caselaw/14993), [shibolet judgments note](https://www.shibolet.com/judgments/%D7%91%D7%99%D7%97%D7%A1-%D7%9C%D7%90%D7%97%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%96%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9F-%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%9D/)
- **Other rulings:** clients have been ordered to pay contractor workers' rights. Courts said that from the law's enactment (Dec 2011), and certainly from its entry into force (June 2012), clients had to supervise and act. Even before the law, case law had imposed client liability in some cases. — [biber-law / irguncleaning "מזמין שירות חויב בתשלום זכויות לעובד"](https://www.biber-law.co.il/work-laws/%D7%9E%D7%96%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9F-%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%99%D7%91-%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%96%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93-%D7%A0/), [irguncleaning](https://irguncleaning.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%96%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9F-%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%99%D7%91-%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%96%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93-%D7%A0/)
- ACRI (the Association for Civil Rights in Israel) reported guards suing both the contractor and the client. — [ACRI 1213](https://law.acri.org.il/he/1213), [ACRI 18950](https://law.acri.org.il/he/18950)
- מינהל הסדרה ואכיפה (Labor Ministry) publishes **annual activity reports** that include sanctions data. — [gov.il annual-activity-reports](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/dynamiccollectors/annual-activity-reports)

### Inferences
- Courts are actively enforcing the soft, process-type duties (complaint channel, reasonable steps). A client with a documented periodic-check trail is materially better placed. This supports steady demand.

### Gaps
- I could not get sanction counts against clients per year from the ministry's annual reports (page blocked).
- I could not confirm the current (2026) indexed sanction amounts.
- I did not find the outcome or amounts in the Shufersal ruling, or the date of the judgment, beyond it being on appeal to the National Labor Court (file year 2021).

## Q6. 2024–2026 changes and whether the state is automating or replacing private checks

### Takeaway
The main recent change is the 2023 hourly-value regulations, fully applicable to existing contracts from 1.1.2025. They **tighten** client obligations (pricing plus quarterly reconciliation) and do not replace checks. I found **no evidence** of a government digital system or bill that would automate or replace private certified payroll checks. The only related digital tool found is the gov.il hourly-value calculator. Searches for a Ministry of Labor automated contractor-payroll check returned only commercial payroll software.

### Cited Findings
- The hourly-value regulations of 2023 apply to all contracts with service contractors in cleaning, guarding and catering from **1 January 2025**, with quarterly reconciliation. — [search summary of goldfarb/shibolet/nevo 217846](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/217846.htm), [ebnlaw Q2 labor-law update](https://www.ebnlaw.co.il/he/q2laborlaw/), [malam-payroll](https://www.malam-payroll.com/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%92%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%94-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94-%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99/)

### Inferences
- **Durability:** the requirement is anchored in a statute (2011), ministerial regulations (2017), newer regulations (2023, effective 2025), Accountant General and Interior Ministry directives, and case law. Every recent change has **added** obligations for clients. Nothing found points to repeal or to replacement by a state system.
- **Automation risk:** it remains plausible in the medium term. Israel's tax authority collects monthly employer reports and many payroll systems are digital. A future state-run data-matching check is conceivable, but I found **no announced project**. Market-side automation (payroll-analytics software) is the more realistic near-term disruption: it lowers the cost of each check rather than removing the legal requirement for a certified checker's signature.

- I could not search Knesset bill databases directly (blocked). Private members' bills from 2024–2026 on client liability, new sectors or digital reporting may exist and were not found.
- I found no information on any Ministry of Labor digital portal for submitting checker reports, or on plans to require contractors to report per-worker payroll data to the ministry.
